ATHENS ALIVE IV — HISTORY & REPAIRS

HOW TO PLAY
1. Open index.html with Chrome or Edge. All gameplay works offline.
2. Start a new game. If you replace index.html on the SAME published website, the updated game can load existing progress. Opening a differently named local file may use separate browser storage.
3. After work and Assembly, press N (or Notes) to complete two DIFFERENT daily history lessons. The topic changes on Days 1–8.
4. After a disaster rescue, visible debris remains until rebuilt. Press R near damaged sites, or click Repairs to see all damaged sites and walk to one.
5. Choose PLAY: Repair the structure, and press SPACE or tap STRIKE in the golden zone three times. Alternatively, free characters can contribute three game drachmas for supplies. Repeat until site damage is zero.
6. Export the student notebook to save all daily prompts, vocabulary, sources and answers.

WHAT CHANGED
• Every day features a new, historically grounded work/social history lesson and civic history lesson (16 different notes in 8-day mode).
• Notes include key terms, historical explanations, source links, and historically focused questions.
• Damage to the Agora, Piraeus, public fountain and farms persists after a rescue and between turns/days until players repair it.
• Repairs include a timing mini-game, building markers, resource costs or labor, and shared city consequences.

HISTORICAL DISCLAIMER
The game simulates Athens around 350 BCE. Disasters and economic figures are fictional teaching models, not actual dated events or exact historical wages. The written history notes have outside scholarly references.

CLASSROOM DEPLOYMENT
Upload index.html from the ZIP as your GitHub Pages home page. No assets, internet access or additional libraries are required to run the game. A browser may restrict save progress in private browsing mode.
